Host economics, ₹ and GB per daypart

Fixed host and guest costs are charged once per activated directed agreement, per representative day. Capacity represents assumed spare roaming GB, not measured Nathu La sector capacity.

Each daypart can use one host, or none. The solver compares 16 choices including no agreement, accounts for fixed costs, and declines individually infeasible contracts. This first-pass model does not combine multiple hosts within the same daypart because joint site-by-site coverage was not measured.

What can the other operators cover?

The displayed shares are mathematical scenarios for the proportion of BSNL's poor-signal samples potentially coinciding with another TSP's acceptable-signal samples, assuming a common sampling frame. The TRAI report does not contain aligned cross-operator measurements, so these are not observed complementarity percentages.
